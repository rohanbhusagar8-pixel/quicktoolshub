BMI Calculator

Check your Body Mass Index based on height and weight.

—
Category—

BMI (Body Mass Index) is calculated as weight in kilograms divided by height in metres squared. It's a quick screening tool: under 18.5 is underweight, 18.5–24.9 is normal, 25–29.9 is overweight, and 30+ is obese. BMI doesn't account for muscle mass, so athletes may see a higher number than expected.

EMI Calculator

Calculate your monthly loan EMI.

—
Total Interest—
Total Payment—

EMI stands for Equated Monthly Installment — the fixed amount you pay every month toward a loan. It's calculated using the formula EMI = P×r×(1+r)^n / ((1+r)^n−1), where P is the principal, r is the monthly interest rate, and n is the number of months. Use this before taking a home, car or personal loan to plan your monthly budget.
